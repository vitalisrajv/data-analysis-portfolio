# Retail Sales Analysis Using SQL

## Project Overview

This project analyses retail sales data using SQL to identify sales trends, product performance, regional performance, and business opportunities.

The analysis uses SQL queries to answer real-world business questions and generate useful insights for decision-making.

## Tools Used
- SQL (SQLite)
- Online SQL Editor
- GitHub

## Dataset
- 120 sales transactions
- 13 columns
- Sales period: January–December 2025
- Currency: Kenyan Shillings (KES)
- ## Key Business Findings

| Analysis | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | KES 7,676,629.59 |
| Total Profit | KES 2,149,391.64 |
| Total Transactions | 120 |
| Average Revenue per Transaction | KES 63,971.91 |
| Profit Margin | 28% |
| Top Revenue Region | Rift Valley |
| Top Revenue Month | October 2025 |
| Top Revenue Category | Electronics |
| Top Revenue Product | Laptop |
| Top Payment Method | M-Pesa |

October 2025 recorded the highest monthly revenue of KES 1,193,429.91, while April 2025 recorded the lowest at KES 130,518.91.
## Business Recommendations

Based on the SQL analysis, the following actions are recommended:

1. **Focus on Electronics:** Electronics generated the highest category revenue. The business should maintain sufficient stock of popular electronics products.

2. **Strengthen Rift Valley Sales:** Rift Valley recorded the highest regional revenue. The business should maintain its strong market presence in this region.

3. **Learn from October Sales:** October had the highest monthly revenue. The business should investigate what contributed to this performance and apply successful strategies in other months.

4. **Promote M-Pesa Payments:** M-Pesa generated the highest revenue by payment method. The business should continue supporting convenient digital payments.

5. **Recognise Top Salespeople:** Esther generated the highest sales revenue. The business can study successful sales approaches and share useful practices with the wider sales team.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how SQL can be used to analyse retail sales data, calculate key business metrics, identify trends, and generate actionable business recommendations.
